Hi, I'm Toshinaki 👋

Creator · Lifelong Learner · Aspiring Digital Nomad

An older single guy. A beginner at investing. Coding is a hobby — mainly because I like to make things, and code is a low-cost, pollution-free way to make them; it's also my job. Working remotely for now, with digital nomad as the goal. Next I want to make games, and try 3D printing. I move too little, so I make myself ride a bike — though I do genuinely like cycling; and lift a little, so I don't look too skinny. I like spicy food, and cilantro. I like horror films. I like learning — all sorts of skills, useful or not. I have a soft spot for the literary girl. Currently trying to break free from “reverse philosophical suicide”.
